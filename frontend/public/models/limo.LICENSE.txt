limo.glb
========

Derived from the AgileX LIMO meshes and URDF in the limo_ros repository:

  Source:      https://github.com/agilexrobotics/limo_ros
  Commit:      4c78efc674cfc154012fe851fbda89c50be5b983 (2025-01-23)
  Path:        limo_description/meshes/{limo_base.dae, limo_base.stl, limo_wheel.dae,
               limo_wheel.stl}
  License:     BSD 3-Clause (reproduced in full below, from the repository's root LICENSE
               file; limo_description/package.xml also declares <license>BSD 3-Clause</license>)
  Copyright:   2021 Agilex Robotics
  Retrieved:   2026-08-31

This .glb is a static, non-articulated conversion of those meshes: the base and four wheels
were assembled per the offsets and origins in limo_description/urdf/limo_four_diff.xacro (the
standard four-wheel differential-drive variant; LIMO also ships mecanum, tracked and Ackermann
variants using the same base mesh with different wheels/hinges, not included here), decimated,
and re-exported. The wheels' real tyre-tread geometry was replaced with a plain cylinder of
matching size (see frontend/scripts/build-limo-glb.mjs for why), and small hardware/fastener
sub-meshes under 15mm were dropped from the base before decimation. See
frontend/scripts/build-limo-glb.mjs and the "Regenerating the robot model" section of
frontend/README.md for the exact process, which is the modification the license below asks be
reflected in redistributed copies.
