jackal.glb
==========

Derived from the Clearpath Jackal meshes and URDF in the jackal repository:

  Source:      https://github.com/jackal/jackal
  Commit:      4ddf9b578bb7abce1115c8dc59d8b7f86aa9268c (2024-05-24)
  Path:        jackal_description/meshes/{jackal-base.stl, jackal-wheel.stl, jackal-fender.stl}
  License:     BSD 3-Clause (reproduced in full below, from the repository's root LICENSE file;
               jackal_description/package.xml also declares <license>BSD</license>)
  Copyright:   2021 Clearpath Robotics Inc.
  Retrieved:   2026-08-31

This .glb is a static, non-articulated conversion of those meshes: the chassis, four wheels,
and front/rear fenders were assembled per the offsets and origins in
jackal_description/urdf/jackal.urdf.xacro, decimated, and re-exported. Optional accessories
(cameras, GPS/antenna mounts, lidar towers, bumpers) are NOT included. See
frontend/scripts/build-jackal-glb.mjs and the "Regenerating the robot model" section of
frontend/README.md for the exact process, which is the modification the license below asks
be reflected in redistributed copies.
