go2.glb
=======

Derived from the Unitree Go2 meshes and URDF in the Unitree Robotics unitree_ros repository:

  Source:      https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros
  Commit:      7d6075f7f58588b189b940130e3edab3c839b2df (fetched 2026-08-31)
  Path:        robots/go2_description/{meshes/*.dae, urdf/go2_description.urdf}

               BSD-3-Clause applies to the meshes.
  Copyright:   HangZhou YuShu TECHNOLOGY CO.,LTD. ("Unitree Robotics"), 2016-2022

This .glb is a static, non-articulated conversion of those meshes: the trunk and the 4 legs'
hip/thigh/calf/foot parts (17 parts total, from 7 unique source .dae files) were assembled
per the joint offsets in go2_description.urdf at the zero joint angle (legs straight down -
the URDF's own rest pose, not a crouched stance), decimated, and re-exported as a single
static mesh. See frontend/scripts/build-go2-glb.mjs for the exact process and
~/reports/M-go2.md for the coordinate/scale/decimation details, which is the modification
this License's clause on redistribution in modified form covers.
